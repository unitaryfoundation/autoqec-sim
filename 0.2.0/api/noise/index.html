    Noise module¶

    autoqec_sim.noise ¶

    Circuit-level noise profiles for stim memory circuits.

    from autoqec_sim.noise import apply, presets
noisy = apply(skeleton, presets.forte(), scale=0.25)

    See README.md for the interface, the profiles table and the sources.

    Correlated dataclass ¶

    Correlated Pauli terms beyond the per-gate channel.

    gate_pairs: ((pauli2, rate), ...) applied after every two-qubit gate on its (first, second) target as CORRELATED_ERROR; with exclusive the terms after the first become ELSE_CORRELATED_ERROR with conditional probabilities so exactly one of them can fire (the DEM conversion of the ELSE form needs approximate_disjoint_errors). crosstalk: for every two-qubit gate (a, b) and every graph neighbour c of a or b outside the gate, CORRELATED_ERROR(crosstalk) with crosstalk_pauli[0] on the gate qubit and crosstalk_pauli[1] on c. Stochastic stand-in for always-on ZZ coupling or spectator drive; needs Profile.graph.

    Idle dataclass ¶

    Idle channel of a layer, applied to every qubit in scope not operated in an active layer.

    kind "duration": PAULI_CHANNEL_1(t / (4 T1), t / (4 T1), t / (2 T2)) with t the layer's wall-clock duration (slowest instruction class present). Pure dephasing from T2 plus a Pauli-twirled amplitude term, linear in t (t << T1, T2). The T2 here is the measured T2 (Hahn or Ramsey as the source states), used as a total dephasing time: the pz formula does not subtract the T1 contribution, which is the same convention as ion_noise. kind "slot": a fixed channel per layer class, gate for layers without a measurement and meas for layers with one. Each is a scalar (emitted as DEPOLARIZE1(rate), the board's form) or a 3-tuple (PAULI_CHANNEL_1(px, py, pz)). kind "none": no idle channel. scope "all": every qubit the circuit uses; "data": qubits 0..n_data-1 (the board rule; apply then needs n_data). bias: for scalar slot rates only, eta of the emitted channel (None or 1/2: DEPOLARIZE1(rate); otherwise PAULI_CHANNEL_1 with pz/(px+py) = eta; inf: Z_ERROR(rate)). Duration idles carry their own T1/T2 structure and tuple slot idles are taken as given.

    Leakage dataclass ¶

    Leakage, in a stim-expressible form.

    Rates: per_meas_layer fires on every qubit not measured in a layer that measures (the mid-circuit-measurement leakage of arXiv:2606.06455 Table V); per_2q fires on both qubits of every two-qubit gate, after the gate's Pauli channel.

    mode "pauli": the erasure-to-Pauli proxy, DEPOLARIZE1(3/4 rate), i.e. the qubit is replaced by the maximally mixed state with probability rate and nothing is heralded. This is the simulation proxy of arXiv:2606.06455 Appendix E. mode "heralded": HERALDED_ERASE(rate), stim's erasure channel with a flag bit in the measurement record; with herald_detectors a DETECTOR on each flag so the DEM carries the herald. This models detected leakage (leakage detection units or post-selection); the board's conformance check does not accept heralded channels, and the DEM conversion needs approximate_disjoint_errors.

    Both are approximations: a leaked qubit is neither erased nor depolarized, it acts as a lost qubit whose gates with neighbours apply non-Pauli errors. Stim has no leaked state.

    Params dataclass ¶

    Rates at scale 1 (probabilities) and durations (seconds).

    p2, p1: total Pauli error per two-qubit gate and per single-qubit unitary, with biases bias2, bias1 (eta = pZ / (pX + pY); 1/2 is depolarizing and is emitted as DEPOLARIZE1/2, any other value as PAULI_CHANNEL_1/2). pauli2, if given, is an explicit 15-vector in stim's PAULI_CHANNEL_2 order that overrides p2 and bias2. p_reset: X_ERROR after R, Z_ERROR after RX. p_meas: flip on M and MX. t_2q, t_1q, t_meas, t_reset: layer durations by instruction class.

    heavy_hex ¶

    heavy_hex(rows: int, cols: int) -> Graph

    Heavy-hexagon lattice of rows x cols hexagons (IBM Eagle and Heron style): a honeycomb with a qubit on every edge, maximum degree 3.

    Construction: rows + 1 horizontal lines of 4 cols + 1 qubits; vertical connector qubits join line i to line i + 1 at horizontal position x with x = 0 mod 4 on even i and x = 2 mod 4 on odd i. Every hexagon then has 6 degree-3 vertices and 6 degree-2 edge qubits (12 qubits). Line qubits are numbered first, row by row, then connectors. IBM's devices trim the outermost lines differently (Eagle 127, Heron r2 156), so sizes match only approximately; the local structure is the same.

    autoqec_sim.noise.profile ¶

    Profile = parameter set + timing + connectivity graph.

    Every probability in a Params is a rate at scale 1; apply multiplies all of them by scale. Durations and coherence times do not scale. A field set to None means "no channel of that class" (the board recipe has no single-qubit-gate channel); 0.0 means a channel that is emitted with probability 0 (so scale sweeps stay structurally identical, as in ion_noise).

    autoqec_sim.noise.apply ¶

    apply(circuit, profile, scale): the noisy circuit of a skeleton.

    Placement per TICK segment of the (re-slotted) skeleton, in this order:

    • each instruction unchanged, followed immediately by its channel: two-qubit gate: DEPOLARIZE2(p2) or PAULI_CHANNEL_2(15 biased args), then the correlated gate-pair terms, then crosstalk terms on graph neighbours, then per-gate leakage; single-qubit unitary: DEPOLARIZE1(p1) / PAULI_CHANNEL_1 / Z_ERROR; R / RX: X_ERROR(p_reset) / Z_ERROR(p_reset); M / MX: flip probability p_meas as the gate argument;
    • if the segment did anything: one idle channel on every in-scope qubit not operated in the segment; then, if the segment measures, the per-measurement-layer leakage term on the same qubits.

    With the ion presets this reproduces ion_noise.apply_ion_noise byte for byte (tests/test_ion_equivalence.py) and with the board preset circuit_tools.apply_noise (tests/test_board_equivalence.py).

    apply ¶

    apply(
    circuit: Circuit,
    profile: Profile,
    scale: float = 1.0,
    *,
    n_data: int | None = None,
    idle_qubits=None,
    check_graph: bool = True,
) -> stim.Circuit

    The noisy circuit for a noiseless skeleton in the board convention.

    n_data is required when the profile's idle scope is "data" (qubits 0..n_data-1 idle, the board rule); idle_qubits overrides the idle set outright. With a connectivity graph on the profile the two-qubit gates are checked first and a ConnectivityError listing every violation is raised (check_graph=False skips it; crosstalk still needs the graph).

    autoqec_sim.noise.timing ¶

    Timing models: how a TICK layer of the skeleton maps onto time slots.

    parallel: every TICK layer is one slot (the board's idealization). sequential: every two-qubit gate is a slot of its own (the chain model of arXiv:2503.22071 and arXiv:2606.06455 Appendix C). gates_per_slot=k: the two-qubit gates of a layer run k at a time, the partial parallelism the ion work found decisive for the idle budget. In every case the non-two-qubit instructions of a layer (resets, single-qubit gates, measurements, annotations) stay together in a first slot, and single-qubit layers are never serialized, so reslot(c, 1) equals ion_noise.serialize_two_qubit(c) instruction for instruction.

    autoqec_sim.noise.channels ¶

    Pauli channels parametrized by a total rate and a bias.

    The bias of a single-qubit Pauli channel is eta = pZ / (pX + pY). The depolarizing channel has eta = 1/2. For two-qubit channels the package uses a product-weight construction: a weight w(I) = w(X) = w(Y) = 1, w(Z) = 2 eta per factor and probability proportional to w(P1) w(P2) for every non-identity pair. Its single-qubit marginals have bias exactly eta and eta = 1/2 gives the uniform two-qubit depolarizing channel. As eta grows the channel concentrates on ZZ (weight 4 eta^2), then equally on the six terms with one Z factor (ZI, IZ, ZX, ZY, XZ, YZ, weight 2 eta), then the eight Z-free terms (weight 1). This is a modelling choice, not a measured decomposition: it does not distinguish ZI from ZX, and it is not the product of two independent single-qubit biased channels (which would have no correlation). Callers with a measured 15-vector pass it through Params.pauli2.
