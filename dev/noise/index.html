    Interface¶

    import stim
from autoqec_sim import noise as nm
from autoqec_sim.noise import presets

noisy = nm.apply(skeleton, presets.forte(), scale=0.25)           # ion, parallel
noisy = nm.apply(skeleton, presets.chain(timing="sequential"), 1.0)
noisy = nm.apply(skeleton, presets.board(), 1.0, n_data=n)         # board recipe
noisy = nm.apply(skeleton, presets.heron(graph=nm.heavy_hex(4, 4)), 1.0)

prof = presets.heron().with_bias(10.0)                            # eta = pZ/(pX+pY)
prof = prof.with_timing(nm.partial(4))                            # 4 two-qubit gates per slot
prof = prof.with_params(correlated=nm.Correlated(crosstalk=1e-4))
f = nm.noisifier(prof, scale=0.5)                                 # circuit -> noisy circuit
dem = nm.dem(noisy)                                               # board options; approximate only if stim requires

    apply(circuit, profile, scale, *, n_data=None, idle_qubits=None, check_graph=True). Every probability of the profile is multiplied by scale; durations and coherence times are not. The input must be noiseless (nm.strip_noise first for a committed board circuit). With a graph on the profile, every two-qubit gate is checked against it and a ConnectivityError listing the violations is raised; check_connectivity returns the list without raising.

    Profile(name, params, timing, graph) bundles:

    Field Content
    Params.p2, bias2, pauli2 two-qubit gate error, its bias, or an explicit 15-vector in stim PAULI_CHANNEL_2 order
    Params.p1, bias1 single-qubit unitary error and bias
    Params.p_reset, p_meas X_ERROR after R and Z_ERROR after RX; flip probability on M and MX
    Params.idle Idle(kind="duration", T1, T2) or Idle(kind="slot", gate, meas) or kind="none"; scope "all" or "data"; optional bias for scalar slot rates
    Params.leakage Leakage(per_meas_layer, per_2q, mode="pauli"/"heralded", herald_detectors)
    Params.correlated Correlated(gate_pairs=((pauli2, rate), ...), exclusive, crosstalk, crosstalk_pauli)
    Params.t_2q, t_1q, t_meas, t_reset layer durations by instruction class, seconds
    Profile.timing PARALLEL, SEQUENTIAL, or partial(k) (Timing(gates_per_slot=k))
    Profile.graph Graph from square, triangular, heavy_hex, complete (ion chain), path, or None for unconstrained

    A rate set to None means no channel of that class (the board recipe has no single-qubit-gate channel); 0.0 emits the channel with probability 0, so scale sweeps keep the instruction structure (as ion_noise does).

    Placement per TICK segment¶

    After the timing rewrite (reslot: the non-two-qubit instructions of a layer stay together in a first slot, then the two-qubit gates k per slot; k=1 equals ion_noise.serialize_two_qubit), each segment emits its instructions unchanged, each followed by its channel: two-qubit gate, then DEPOLARIZE2(p2) or PAULI_CHANNEL_2, then the gate_pairs terms, then crosstalk terms, then per-gate leakage; single-qubit unitary, then DEPOLARIZE1(p1), PAULI_CHANNEL_1, or Z_ERROR (infinite bias); reset, then its flip; measurement with p_meas as the gate argument. If the segment did anything, one idle channel goes on every in-scope qubit not operated in it, and, if the segment measures, the per-measurement-layer leakage term on the same qubits. The idle duration is the slowest instruction class present in the layer.

    Channels¶

    Single-qubit bias: px = py = p / (2 (1 + eta)), pz = p eta / (1 + eta); eta = 1/2 is depolarizing and is emitted as DEPOLARIZE1(p).

    Two-qubit bias (product-weight construction): weights w(I) = w(X) = w(Y) = 1, w(Z) = 2 eta, probability of the pair P1 P2 proportional to w(P1) w(P2) over the 15 non-identity pairs, total p. Each single-qubit marginal has bias exactly eta; eta = 1/2 is the uniform depolarizing channel and is emitted as DEPOLARIZE2(p). At large eta the mass sits on ZZ (weight 4 eta^2), then equally on the six pairs with one Z factor (2 eta), then the eight Z-free pairs. This is a modelling choice with no measured decomposition behind it (no IonQ or IBM Pauli-twirled two-qubit decomposition is public); a measured 15-vector goes in Params.pauli2.

    Correlated: gate_pairs are CORRELATED_ERROR lines after every two-qubit gate on its two targets, independent by default; exclusive=True chains them with ELSE_CORRELATED_ERROR using conditional probabilities q_i = p_i / (1 - sum_{j<i} p_j). Crosstalk: for every two-qubit gate (a, b) and every graph neighbour c of a or b outside the gate, one CORRELATED_ERROR(rate) with crosstalk_pauli[0] on the gate qubit and crosstalk_pauli[1] on c (default ZZ, a stochastic stand-in for always-on ZZ coupling or spectator drive). Spectators that are themselves in another gate of the layer are included.

    Leakage: mode="pauli" is the erasure-to-Pauli proxy, DEPOLARIZE1(3/4 rate) (the qubit replaced by the maximally mixed state with probability rate, the simulation proxy of 1, Appendix E). mode="heralded" is HERALDED_ERASE(rate), stim's erasure with a flag bit in the measurement record, plus a DETECTOR per flag when herald_detectors (default), so the DEM carries the herald; the skeleton's rec[-k] references are rewritten around the extra records and nm.strip_noise removes the heralds again. Both are approximations: stim has no leaked state, and a leaked qubit is neither erased nor depolarized (its later gates apply non-Pauli errors on neighbours).

    DEM exactness¶

    The board's pinned derivation (derive_dem, approximate_disjoint_errors False) converts DEPOLARIZE1/2, X_ERROR, Z_ERROR, PAULI_CHANNEL_1 with three positive components, and independent CORRELATED_ERROR terms exactly. It refuses PAULI_CHANNEL_2 (any two-qubit bias other than 1/2, or pauli2), ELSE_CORRELATED_ERROR, HERALDED_ERASE, and a PAULI_CHANNEL_1 with a zero component. Profile.dem_exact says which case a profile is in; nm.dem(circuit) tries the exact conversion and falls back to approximate_disjoint_errors=True (error O(p^2) in the merged probabilities, mechanism set unchanged), or raises with approximate=False. Board conformance (noise_recipe_errors) accepts only the board profile's output.

    Topologies¶

    Builder Graph Notes
    heavy_hex(rows, cols) honeycomb of rows x cols hexagons with a qubit on every edge; max degree 3 IBM Eagle and Heron style. rows + 1 lines of 4 cols + 1 qubits plus connectors; 12 qubits per hexagon. IBM trims the outer lines differently (Eagle 127, Heron r2 156), so sizes match approximately
    square(rows, cols) degree 4 Google Sycamore and Willow style
    triangular(rows, cols) square plus one diagonal per cell, degree 6
    complete(n) (ion_chain) all-to-all one trapped-ion chain; presets.forte_graph(36)
    path(n) nearest-neighbour line

    check_connectivity(circuit, graph, layout=None) returns one Violation (layer, gate, qubits, reason) per two-qubit gate whose mapped pair is not an edge or touches a node outside the graph; apply raises ConnectivityError with the list. The [[9,1,3]] one-round circuits have 24 violations on any heavy-hex graph with the identity layout (the circuit uses one ancilla per check with all-to-all placement); a layout mapping is the caller's job.

    Use in the circuit-level reward¶

    circuit_noise.resolve_noise_model accepts a autoqec_sim.noise.Profile (presets.forte(), presets.chain(), presets.board(), presets.heron(), presets.depolarizing(p), or any with_bias, with_timing, with_params variant), a preset name, or a bare stim.Circuit -> stim.Circuit callable. The encoding gate list is laid out as a TICK-layered skeleton (as-soon-as-possible scheduling) so the profile's timing model and idle channel apply; every noise instruction the profile emits becomes a fault mechanism, propagated through the rest of the circuit (a GF(2) product against the symplectic map of the circuit suffix, which fault_set_reference reproduces with stim's PauliString.after) and scored with the Knill-Laflamme kernel as -(undetected mass), normalized by the total mass. A profile without a graph takes the environment's graph; a profile whose graph differs is refused, and a two-qubit gate off the graph raises ConnectivityError (or scores violation_reward with on_violation="penalize"). dem_undetected_mass cross-checks the result against stim's detector error model to O(p^2). Use through make_circuit_level_reward(env, profile) as a terminal reward (reward_mode="sparse"); it is per circuit, not batched.
