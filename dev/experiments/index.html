    Experiments¶

    Each experiment asks one question about code discovery or code evaluation, runs from experiments/<name>/run.py in the repository (uv run python -m experiments.<name>.run from the repository root; the code is not part of the installed package), and keeps its write-up in experiments/<name>/REPORT.md next to it (raw runs under experiments/<name>/runs/ are not tracked). Each page gives the question, the result with its figure, and the commands that reproduce one run.

    Experiment Question Result
    Noise-aware discovery Does the code PPO discovers depend on the noise profile it trains against? Yes, by 1.3x to 3.4x in undetected fault mass, through the encoder's fault propagation rather than the code's X/Z structure
    Memory objective runs Does the closed-form proxy of the memory objective rank codes like a sampled decoder? Spearman 0.78 to 0.90 against BP+OSD on 32 random codes; XZZX preferred under Z bias
    RL scaling probe Why does PPO miss the [[16,1,4]] code, and what is the cheapest fix? Exploration; a permutation-equivariant policy finds it in 0.2M to 0.26M env steps on every seed
    Three-topology matrix Does PPO reach distance 3 on square, triangular, and heavy-hex lattices? 18 of 18 runs, at 6k to 345k env steps
    Evolutionary search against PPO Does the package's evolutionary search match PPO at the same budget? 17 of 18 runs succeed; PPO needs 1.4x to 6x fewer env steps, the evolutionary search far less wall time
    QDX's targets How many of the 50 targets QDX publishes does this PPO reach in 10 minutes each? 24 of the 45 run: 22 of 36 at d = 3, 2 of 9 at d = 4
    SAT-designed codes Does a code-capacity ranking of fifteen SAT-designed codes survive circuit-level noise? Barely: Spearman 0.08 to 0.52; XZZX beats or ties every one of them
    SAT population The same question over 2,150 codes No: Spearman 0.00 to 0.21; the best codes sit 30 to 40 percent below XZZX
    Proxy against Tesseract Does the shot-free proxy rank distance-5 codes like a near-maximum-likelihood decoder? Yes: Spearman 0.89 and 0.93 over 21 codes at three and five rounds, about 30 percent low in level at 1.7 to 2.8 expected faults per shot, at a fifth of the cost of 50,000 shots
    Published experiments How far is the evaluator from five published per-round logical error rates, on its own circuit with each paper's parameters? Below the paper's number in all nine rows: 0.4 to 0.9 of it for the surface, toric, bicycle, and heavy-hex rows, 0.2 to 0.45 for Steane, where the circuits differ most; matching on the Google circuits within 2 to 24 percent of the papers' matching figures

    Every number on these pages is copied from the experiment's report, which regenerates from the runs with the module's report command; the figures regenerate with its figure command (or report, for the older experiments). The memory-objective runs recorded here used the batched extraction schedule, the only one before the interleaved schedule became the default; extraction="batched" reproduces them. Where a case is named Forte, it is the package's Forte preset, a noise model assembled from public specifications, not a measurement on a device.
