    heron-r2 sources, accessed 2026-09-20. IBM Heron r2: 156 fixed-frequency transmons, tunable couplers, heavy-hex lattice, native gates CZ, RZ, SX, X, measurement, reset (measurement followed by a conditional X).

    Parameter Value Source and status
    p2 3.5e-3 5, Section IV.1.2: median two-qubit error "approximately 0.35%" on Heron-class devices. Cross-check: IBM Quantum Platform table for ibm_fez quotes a 2.77e-3 median, and 2.85e-3 is quoted for Heron r2 in secondary sources attributing it to 6 (not verified against that primary)
    p1 2.0e-4 5, Table 1, fitted single-qubit depolarizing 0.02%. A fitted model value, not an RB median; the SX medians quoted for Heron executions elsewhere (2.2e-4) agree
    p_meas 1.5e-2 IBM Quantum Platform ibm_fez median readout assignment error 1.55e-2 as quoted in secondary sources on 2026-09-20. 5, Table 1, fits 1.2% quantum plus 4.2% classical measurement error; the assignment error is used
    p_reset 1.5e-2 ASSUMPTION. IBM reset is measure plus conditional X (7, appendix), so its flip rate is taken equal to the readout error. 5, Table 1, fits 7.5% reset noise, which would make reset the dominant term; not adopted without a characterization
    T1, T2 213 us, 120 us 5, Section IV.1.2, "on the best Heron device". Medians of 218 us and 264 us are quoted for Heron r2 in secondary sources; the primary-paper pair is used
    t_2q 68 ns CZ duration for Heron r2, quoted in secondary sources (Legato.jl issue 15, attributing 6). Not verified against the primary
    t_1q 32 ns 7, appendix: "an X gate is 32 ns = 8 dt" on ibm_aachen (Heron r2)
    t_meas 3.5 us 5, Section IV.1.2: measurement "between 3000 and 4000 ns"; midpoint, ASSUMPTION. Other Heron devices quote 1.3 to 2.6 us readout lengths
    t_reset t_meas + t_1q ASSUMPTION from the measure-plus-X construction
    bias 1/2 ASSUMPTION: no public Pauli decomposition of CZ or SX on Heron
    leakage 0 not sourced: no published CZ leakage rate for Heron; left at 0 and stated
    crosstalk 0 7 names coherent ZZ crosstalk as the dominant term dynamical decoupling suppresses, with no rate; set Correlated(crosstalk=...) per run

    The Heron idle term at these numbers is small per layer (CZ layer pz = 2.8e-4, meas layer pz = 1.5e-2 at scale 1) because T2 is 1800 CZ durations; the measurement layer dominates the idle budget, the reverse of the Forte set.

    Parameters that are assumptions¶

    Forte and chain sets: (SPAM split, t_meas, t_reset, two-qubit Pauli structure, Yb+ leakage, simultaneous gate count; chain p2 read off a figure, p_reset, X/Y idle split). Heron set: p_reset, t_meas (midpoint of a stated range), t_reset, the Pauli bias of CZ and SX, CZ leakage, and the ZZ crosstalk rate; t_2q = 68 ns and the ibm_fez medians are from secondary quotations of IBM's device table, not from a paper I could open. The two-qubit biased channel's product-weight form is a construction, not a measurement, for every preset.
